    The streak counts days where you finish at least 3 of the first 6 items.

    Why the session is built this way

    1. Ladder volume on its own plateaus. At a high level you already know the game. What's left to gain is in the few turns per game you get wrong, and you only find those by logging them and looking at them later.

    2. Daily drills build automatic knowledge. Type matchups, speed tiers, items and mechanics should be instant, so your thinking time goes to reads and planning. Five minutes a day of drills keeps it sharp, and missed questions come back more often.

    3. One focus per week. When you try to fix everything at once, nothing changes. Pick one habit and run it every game until it's automatic. The weekly review shows you which one to pick next.

    4. Plan before turn 1. Most Bo1 losses are decided at team preview. Saying your bring-4, lead and win condition out loud makes the decision deliberate, and your log shows whether the plan was the problem.

    5. Review a loss turn by turn. You remember the crit, not the turn 2 Protect that set it up. Rewatching finds the turn the game actually turned.

    6. Predict top players. Calling a top player's bring-4 and leads before they're revealed trains the read you need at preview.

    7. Bo3 every week. Ladder is Bo1, but big events are Bo3. Games 2 and 3 are about adapting to what you saw, and you can't practice that on ladder.

        How Worlds invites work

        2027 season rules
        • Each season runs from September to that year's Worlds in August. Championship Points (CP) reset every season.
        • Winning a Regional gives a Worlds invite plus 350 CP.
        • Your best 5 Regional and International finishes count. Locals count too: League Challenge 1st is 15 CP and League Cup 1st is 50 CP, with your best 4 of each counting.
        • Online Global Challenges also award CP, counted by rating zone.

        These rules change between seasons. Check the current numbers before you plan around them: Victory Road season structure.

        2027 season events: North America and majors

        Treat these as reps, not deadlines

        Every big event you play, even a 3–4 finish, is Bo3 experience against strong players. Find League Challenges and Cups near you with the Play! Pokémon event locator. Dates are from Victory Road's calendar as of Nov 12, 2026, so check them before booking travel.

        Teambuilding, on the back burner

        For now, save every team you play here, even rentals and copied teams. Write down why it works and what it struggles with. Your log tracks each team's record, so when you start building your own you'll have a record of which ideas actually won.

        Not set up

        Your data saves on this device automatically. To use the same data on your phone and computer, connect a GitHub token. VGC Lab keeps your data in one private gist on your GitHub account, and nothing else on your account is touched.

        1. On github.com, open Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens, and choose Generate new token.
        2. Name it "VGC Lab". Under Account permissions, set Gists to Read and write. Leave everything else off.
        3. Generate it, copy it, and paste it below. Do the same on each device. They'll all find the same gist.
